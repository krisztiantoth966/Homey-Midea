Control Midea-family air conditioners directly over the local network. Air conditioners are found automatically on the local network; SmartHome is needed only once for a new V3 air conditioner to obtain its LAN key. Remote-control changes, live power and a filter-cleaning reminder are reported to Homey. Homey Energy uses a monotonic hybrid meter that integrates live power during normal operation and uses native Midea counters to recover gaps.
